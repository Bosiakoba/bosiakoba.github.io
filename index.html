Kantesh Kumar, MBBS

Physician-researcher · MPH candidate, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health

I am a physician-researcher and MPH candidate in Quantitative Methods at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, where I am a Presidential Scholar (expected graduation May 2027). I completed my MBBS at Aga Khan University in 2023 and have since worked on trauma and emergency care research at the Aga Khan University Centre of Excellence for Trauma & Emergencies in Karachi. My work focuses on prehospital emergency care, trauma outcomes, traumatic brain injury, climate and health, and disaster preparedness in low- and middle-income countries. I am interested in a surgery residency and welcome conversations with colleagues and potential collaborators.
